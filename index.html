Research

2026 – Present   /   University of Notre Dame

Local Convergence Analysis of CP-ALS for Tensor Completion

Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Yuefeng Han

I aim to develop a rigorous proof of the fast convergence of the synchronous CP-ALS algorithm for noisy tensor completion under a fixed Bernoulli observation mask. The project is inspired by Tang et al., Revisit CP Tensor Decomposition: Statistical Optimality and Fast Convergence. I study observation-dependent row-wise least-squares updates, conditioning of random Gram matrices, and iterate–sample dependence. Numerical experiments help identify regimes of fast recovery and instability.

Summer 2025   /   The University of Hong Kong

Certifying Optimality in Nonconvex Robust PCA

Summer Research Fellow · Advisor: Prof. Lexiao Lai

For an ℓ₁-loss, low-rank factorized robust PCA objective, I studied first-order optimality at ground-truth factorizations. The analysis uses low-rank tangent-space projections, incoherence assumptions, and concentration estimates to derive conditions certifying Clarke criticality with high probability. Our paper also studies the contrasting local geometry of exact-rank and overparameterized factorizations.
