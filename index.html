Research Projects

Local Convergence Analysis of CP-ALS for Tensor Completion

Jun 2026 – present

University of Notre Dame · Advisor: Prof. Yuefeng Han

I study synchronous CP alternating least squares for noisy tensor completion under a fixed Bernoulli observation mask. The project examines masked least-squares updates, random Gram matrix conditioning, sampling concentration, and the dependence between iterates and observed entries, supported by numerical experiments.

Certifying Optimality in Nonconvex Robust PCA

Jun – Aug 2025

The University of Hong Kong · Advisor: Prof. Lexiao Lai

I investigated first-order optimality conditions for an ℓ₁-factorized robust PCA formulation. Using tangent-space bounds and concentration inequalities, this work establishes sufficient rank and corruption-sparsity conditions under which ground-truth factorizations are Clarke critical points with high probability.

Preprint · Research poster presented at HKU on October 31, 2025.

Quantitative Trading Simulation

The University of Hong Kong · Advisor: Dr. Eric Ah Lik Li
